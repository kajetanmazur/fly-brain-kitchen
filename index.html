For grown-ups: where this comes from Every bar and dot is replayed from a real simulation run: Shiu et al. 2024 whole-brain leaky integrate-and-fire model on the FlyWire v783 connectome (~139k neurons), averaged over trials; neuron positions from FlyWire v783. The game only decides which run to play (what touched the fly) and where the fly walks. Brain time is slowed 3×. The white line (50 Hz) is our game rule. The eye view is simple geometry, not a brain model. Smells are left out: in this model every odour sets off a seizure-like storm across ~8,700 neurons.
